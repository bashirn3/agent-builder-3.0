## Why scenarios were flagged

- **Timeouts under load.** With 9 to 12 parallel runs a reply took about 40 s (p50) and the provider or the Muster connection timed out, so the agent sent its fallback reply. The two price questions answered with "can't verify the price, call 0306 100 100" were price lookups that timed out; the agent handled that correctly.
- **Slot contention.** Parallel booking runs took the same slot. Serial reruns booked cleanly.
- **Checks that were too strict** (fixed in `tests/`):
  - "Ylihuomenna" (the day after tomorrow) answered from `get_station_info` with the right Friday times: date checks now accept the `get_station_info` date.
  - "Any times on 10.1?" answered with a clarifying question: the check now accepts a question or times.
  - The bot refused to confirm the customer's 25 € price while giving the real 76 €: the check now only fails when the bot accepts the wrong price.
  - "Kiva kuulla!" as a whole reply to "hyvää" was counted as a filler opener: replies that are only that phrase are no longer flagged.
